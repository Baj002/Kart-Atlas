# Kart Atlas privacy policy — owner review draft

Not ready to publish: add the confirmed publisher name, support contact, public policy URL and effective date after reviewing the final app and every included service.

Kart Atlas lets people discover public kart races and save races on their device. No Kart Atlas login is required. The prepared app does not include advertising or an analytics SDK.

Saved races, reminder choices, scheduled notification details and cached public race data are stored on the device. Phone reminder permission is optional. Notification scheduling is local; no saved-race list is sent to a Kart Atlas push-notification server. Device backup settings may affect local app data. Uninstalling the app removes its local app data; files exported to a calendar or another app must be managed there.

When connected, the app requests public race and club data from the Kart Atlas GitHub repository using GitHub's raw-content hosting. The map requests tiles from CARTO and displays OpenStreetMap attribution. These network providers receive connection information such as IP addresses and request metadata according to their own policies. The publisher must review these providers' current practices when completing store privacy declarations; the absence of an analytics SDK alone is not sufficient to answer every store question.

Registration websites open separately. Data users submit there, including payment details, is handled by the organizer or provider under its own policies. Kart Atlas does not process registration payments.

Users can remove saved races, disable reminders or revoke notification permission in device settings. Already-exported calendar reminders and external calendar subscriptions must be removed in the user's calendar application.

No precise device location permission is requested. Map pins represent approximate published event locations, not the user's live location.
