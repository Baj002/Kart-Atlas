## Full description
Find your next kart race across Canada, the United States and Mexico.

Kart Atlas brings competition circuit karting calendars into one searchable directory. Discover local club races, regional championships and national events without checking every organizer individually.

• Search by date, location, organizer and racing level.
• Browse races on a map or monthly calendar.
• Save races to plan your season.
• Check verified registration links, status and online deadlines where available.
• Set optional phone reminders for published registration deadlines.
• Share race information and calendar files.
• Browse a bundled directory offline and refresh race data when connected.

Designed for competition karting, including Briggs LO206, Rotax, ROK, shifter, IAME and comparable classes. Races remain discoverable when class information has not been published.

Kart Atlas is an independent discovery service. Coverage is expanding, and organizer schedules can change. Confirm dates, eligibility, entry availability and fees with the organizer. Registration and payment take place with the organizer or their registration provider.

Phone reminders use the last synced deadline. Open Kart Atlas to refresh changes; notification permissions and device power settings affect delivery.

## Review notes
No account or payment is required to use Kart Atlas. Save a race with a published deadline, open Manage reminders, and explicitly enable phone reminders to test notification permission and scheduling. The permission prompt is not shown on launch. Unsave a race or turn off its reminders to cancel its scheduled alerts. The app schedules at most 60 pending phone alerts, prioritizing the nearest deadlines.

External registration links open in the system browser experience. The app does not sell race entries, process payments, collect location permission or offer an entrant directory. The map uses approximate city positions.
