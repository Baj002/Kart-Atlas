Website notification privacy

Website notifications are optional. Browsing and calendar exports do not require enabling them.

What enabling notifications sends

Your browser creates a device-specific notification address and encryption keys. Kart Atlas stores these privately with the race IDs you select for reminders, your reminder lead time, and timestamps needed to deliver alerts. Your full local saved-race list is not sent unless every saved race is included. No name, email, precise location or registration/payment details are requested.

How it works

Kart Atlas checks its published race data for verified deadlines and sends encrypted reminders through your browser’s notification provider, such as Apple, Google or Mozilla. Cloudflare hosts the notification service and database. These providers receive network requests, including IP addresses, under their own policies. Notification text can appear on your lock screen; you control preview and notification settings on your phone.

Controls and deletion

Use Manage reminders to change your lead time, exclude a race, or turn website notifications off. Turning them off unsubscribes this browser and requests deletion of its server subscription and queued reminders. Offline deletion retries when you reconnect and reopen Kart Atlas. Clearing browser data or revoking permission can leave a server record until the provider rejects it or it expires. Subscriptions expire after 180 days without syncing; reopening Kart Atlas refreshes that period. Delivery records are removed within 30 days after an alert expires. Operational records contain counts, not notification addresses or race selections.

Limitations

Alerts use published evidence and may be delayed or unavailable. Unknown deadlines do not produce alerts. Phone connectivity and notification settings affect delivery. Always confirm registration with the organizer. Calendar subscriptions are managed separately in your calendar app.
