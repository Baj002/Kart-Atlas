2. Florida Winter Tour 2027 Rounds 2 and 3 have published dates but no confirmed venue in the indexed records; remain marked TBA.
3. A prior ROK Sonoma 2027 announcement conflicts with the organizer's current calendar; the older bulletin's automated imports were disabled and dates corrected using the current calendar.
4. Seventeen states lack state-specific official monitoring sources in the current inventory: AL, AK, DE, HI, KY, LA, ME, MA, MN, MS, MT, NM, ND, RI, SD, VA, WY. National series may still visit some of these states; do not equate missing source with no racing.
5. Some sources are historical or require eligibility review (e.g., Nebraska karting on a larger circuit; Vermont organizer still displays a 2025 schedule). Do not auto-publish from these until dates and karting format are independently verified.
6. Repository changes are committed, but public Cloudflare deployment and GitHub CI test results were not independently verified in this run.
